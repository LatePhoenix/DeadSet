# Winterland Arena, December 31, 1978

The Grateful Dead’s New Year’s Eve 1978 concert was the closing night of Winterland Arena in San Francisco (Bill Graham).

## Why it matters

Winterland is central to late-1970s Dead history (including the Scarlet → Fire debut night earlier in 1977). Prefer the dead.net show page and Archive transfers for setlist and tape details rather than secondary “best of” lists.
