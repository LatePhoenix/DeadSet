# Keyboard lineage

DeadSet lists the commonly cited keyboard succession for the Grateful Dead. Exact join/leave dates vary by source; this guide names people only and points to dead.net member pages.

## Sequence (names only)

1. Ron “Pigpen” McKernan
2. Tom Constanten
3. Keith Godchaux
4. Brent Mydland
5. Vince Welnick
6. Bruce Hornsby (part-time / later periods)

## Note

Do not treat this list as a timeline of exclusive tenure — Constanten overlapped with Pigpen, Hornsby with Welnick, and guest appearances complicate any single chart. Follow the linked band pages for sourced biography.
