# Wall of Sound

The Wall of Sound was a large concert PA designed for the Grateful Dead. Lead designer **Owsley “Bear” Stanley** worked with the band’s sound crew and Alembic collaborators.

## Debut

The completed system made its touring debut on **March 23, 1974** at the Cow Palace in Daly City, California. Speaker and wattage counts vary across secondary sources; this guide does not pick a single number when sources disagree.

## Context

The system was used through much of 1974 before the band’s mid-decade touring hiatus. For deeper engineering detail, follow the linked sources rather than paraphrasing disputed figures.
