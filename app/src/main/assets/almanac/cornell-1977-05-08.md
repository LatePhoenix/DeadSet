# Barton Hall, May 8, 1977

The Grateful Dead played Barton Hall at Cornell University in Ithaca, New York on May 8, 1977.

## Why it matters

The recording of this concert was added to the Library of Congress National Recording Registry in **2011**. That listing is the primary sourced claim for this spotlight — not later fan lore about “best ever.”

## Listen

Archive.org hosts many transfers of the show in the Grateful Dead collection. Prefer named sources over rumor when describing tape lineage.
